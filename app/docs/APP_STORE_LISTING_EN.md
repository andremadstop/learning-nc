## Full Description

### Learning — Spaced Repetition for Nextcloud

Master any subject with scientifically-proven spaced repetition. **Learning** brings the Leitner 5-box system to your Nextcloud, so your study data stays on your server.

#### Why Learning?

- **Privacy-First**: Your questions, answers, and progress never leave your server
- **Native Integration**: Dashboard widget, user sharing, Nextcloud authentication
- **Proven Method**: The Leitner system schedules reviews at increasing intervals (1, 3, 7, 14 days)
- **Team-Ready**: Share pools with colleagues for collaborative learning

#### Features

**Question Pools**
Create multiple pools for different subjects. Each question supports 2-6 multiple choice answers with optional explanations and difficulty levels.

**Leitner Spaced Repetition**
Questions start in Box 1. Answer correctly and they move up through 5 boxes with increasing review intervals. Get it wrong and it goes back to Box 1. Questions in Box 5 are mastered.

**Training Mode**
Quick quiz sessions with immediate feedback and score tracking. Perfect for a daily review or exam prep.

**Pool Sharing**
Share your pools with other Nextcloud users. Choose between read-only access (study only) or edit access (add questions).

**CSV & JSON Import**
Import hundreds of questions at once from CSV or JSON files. Great for converting existing study materials.

**Dashboard Widget**
See how many questions are due at a glance, right from your Nextcloud Dashboard.

**Exam Mode**
Timed exams with configurable question count and strict scoring. Simulate real test conditions.

**True/False Mode**
Swipe-based flashcard review optimized for mobile. Swipe right for correct, left for wrong.

**Smart Queue**
One click on "Learn Now" — all due cards from every pool in one prioritized session. Lower boxes and oldest overdue cards come first.

**Gamification**
Earn XP, level up, unlock 14 achievements. Level-up celebrations with animations. Daily streaks keep you motivated.

**Daily Goal**
Set a configurable daily review target (default: 20 cards) with a visual progress ring. +10 XP bonus when you hit your daily goal.

**Course Management**
Instructors can create courses, assign question pools, enroll students, and track their progress from a dedicated dashboard. Leaderboard with XP ranking and student detail drill-down.

**Multi-Language Support**
Translate questions and answers into any language. Filter by language during training sessions.

**Question Search**
Full-text search across all your pools. Find any question instantly.

**Analytics Dashboard**
Detailed per-pool statistics with accuracy trends and study history.

**Mobile-Friendly**
Fully responsive design with touch-optimized buttons and swipe gestures. Study on your phone during your commute.

#### Use Cases

- **IT Certification**: CompTIA A+/Network+/Security+, Cisco CCNA, AWS
- **Compliance Training**: GDPR, ISO 27001, workplace safety
- **University**: Exam preparation, vocabulary, formulas
- **Onboarding**: Company processes, product knowledge
- **Languages**: Vocabulary and grammar rules

#### Requirements

- Nextcloud 29, 30, or 31
- PHP 8.1+
- PostgreSQL 13+ or MySQL 8+
