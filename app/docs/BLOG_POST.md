# Introducing Learning: Spaced Repetition for Nextcloud

*Study smarter, not harder — with your data on your own server.*

---

I built **Learning** because I wanted a simple, self-hosted flashcard app with proper spaced repetition. Tools like Anki are great, but they run locally and don't integrate with the rest of your infrastructure. Cloud services like Quizlet hold your data hostage. If you already run Nextcloud, why not keep your learning data there too?

## What it does

Learning is a Nextcloud app that lets you create multiple choice question pools and study them using the **Leitner 5-box system** — a scientifically-proven method where questions you know well are reviewed less often, while questions you struggle with come back more frequently.

**The key features:**

- **Question Pools** with multiple choice answers, explanations, and difficulty levels
- **Leitner Boxes** with automatic scheduling (review after 1, 3, 7, and 14 days)
- **Training Mode** for quick quiz sessions with instant feedback
- **Pool Sharing** with other Nextcloud users (read-only or edit access)
- **CSV/JSON Import** to bulk-load existing question sets
- **Dashboard Widget** showing due questions at a glance
- **Mobile-friendly** responsive design

## Who it's for

- **IT teams** studying for certifications (CompTIA, Cisco, AWS)
- **Companies** running compliance training (GDPR, ISO 27001)
- **Students** preparing for exams
- **Anyone** who wants to remember what they learn

## Privacy-first

Your questions, answers, and progress stay on your Nextcloud server. No external services, no tracking, no data leaving your infrastructure. AGPL-3.0 licensed.

## Get started

Install from the Nextcloud App Store, create a pool, add some questions (or import from CSV), and start learning. The Leitner system takes care of scheduling — just review your due questions daily.
